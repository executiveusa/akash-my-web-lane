# The Scout Method: How One Agency Found 200 Warm Leads Without Ads

## The Problem With Cold Outreach

Most agency growth advice sounds like this: post on LinkedIn, run Google Ads, attend local events, build referral partnerships.

All of that works. Eventually. Slowly. Expensively.

Here's a different approach: go find the exact people who have a problem you can demonstrably solve, show them you've already solved it, and ask them if they want it.

That's the Scout Method.

---

## What the Scout Agent Does

The Scout Agent is a feature in Akash Engine's Agency plan that runs automatically every night.

Here's what it does:

1. **Targets a geography + industry you specify.** "Dental clinics within 30 miles of Dallas" or "law firms in Chicago."

2. **Finds their websites.** It cross-references business directories, Google Maps, and web crawl data to find active business websites.

3. **Runs Lighthouse audits.** For each site it finds, it runs performance, SEO, and Core Web Vitals checks.

4. **Identifies hot prospects.** Sites with Lighthouse scores below 65 are flagged as "hot" — they have a severe, demonstrable problem.

5. **Builds preview migrations.** For the top hot prospects, it automatically runs a migration and builds a preview of what their site would look like on Akash Engine infrastructure.

6. **Enriches contact data.** Business email, phone, owner name (where available).

7. **Prepares your outreach.** You wake up to a dashboard showing "47 leads found, 12 previews built, here are the personalized outreach drafts."

You didn't run a single ad. You haven't sent a cold email yet. But you have 12 people whose new website is already built and waiting for them.

**Why it works:**
- The specific score (not "your site is slow") proves you actually looked
- The preview link shows them the result before they commit
- "$499 and free hosting forever" eliminates the ongoing cost fear
- No pushy close — just "let me know if you have questions"

## The Three Things That Made the Difference

### 1. The Preview Link is the Product

Marcus said this clearly: "If I just send an email saying 'I can improve your site,' I get maybe 2% response. When I send an email with a working preview link of their new site, I get 21%."

The preview isn't a mockup. It's a real, working Astro site with their actual content, loading in under a second. The prospect clicks the link and sees their business, but faster and cleaner.

That's a fundamentally different sales conversation. You're not pitching a promise. You're showing the delivery.

### 2. The Dental Clinic Segment Converts Fastest

Marcus found that dental and medical offices were the most responsive, by far. His theory: "They're focused on patients, not technology. When their site is broken, they have no idea what to do. Someone showing up with a fix, saying it's $499 and takes a week — that's a huge relief."

The law firm segment converted more slowly but at higher values: attorneys understood the ROI language (lost clients from bad SEO = quantifiable lost revenue) and were more willing to invest in the AI services retainer.

Restaurants were the hardest: high turnover in ownership, lower margins, more price sensitivity. But when they converted, the referral networks were strong.

### 3. Showing the Lighthouse Score Before the Preview

Marcus's first batch had a 9% response rate. His second batch had 21%. The difference: he added the Lighthouse score to the subject line.

"Your dental office website scored 38/100 on Google's performance test" is a more compelling subject line than "Your website could be faster."

The specific number creates urgency. It implies that other dental offices scored higher. It references Google, which is already trusted.

## How to Set Up Your Own Scout Pipeline

**Step 1:** Identify your target segment. Choose one geography (your metro area or a specific city) and two industries where you have at least one existing client or case study.

**Step 2:** Set up the Scout Agent. In your Akash Engine Agency dashboard, configure the geographic radius, industry categories, and Lighthouse score thresholds.

**Step 3:** Let it run for 3 nights. On day 4, you'll have your first batch of leads and previews.

**Step 4:** Personalize and send. Don't blast. Spend 2-3 minutes per email adding one specific detail about their business ("I noticed you're listed as a criminal defense firm" or "I see you have two locations").

**Step 5:** Follow up once. Seven days after your first email, a single follow-up: "Wanted to make sure you saw the preview I built for you — link here." No apology. No pressure.

**Step 6:** Track and optimize. After your first 20 sends, you'll know your response rate. If it's under 10%, adjust the subject line and first paragraph. If it's above 15%, you've found your formula.

## What Scout Can't Do

Honest limitations:

- **WooCommerce stores are excluded from migration.** If Scout finds a high-traffic ecommerce site, the outreach model doesn't apply — migration isn't the right tool for them.

- **Some businesses have technical redirects or custom functionality** that needs evaluation before migration. Scout flags these, but they require manual assessment.

- **Response rate drops if you over-saturate a market.** If you send 200 emails per week in a small market, people start talking. Pace your outreach.

- **It's a top-of-funnel tool.** Scout finds and warms the lead. Closing still requires a human. The 22-minute sales call is still your job.
